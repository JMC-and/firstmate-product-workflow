# Design provenance and MIT notices

This native implementation re-expresses selected interview, specification, scenario and evidence concepts from these verified research snapshots.
It imports no code, package, installer, hook, orchestrator or automatic updater from them and claims no endorsement.
The MIT notices are retained conservatively for the adapted planning guidance.

- Matt Pocock, [skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/4ae2317ddb22b046e26455e4f9c9b3aaaf62212d): dependency-aware interview rounds, factual research versus owner decisions, specification synthesis and vertical slicing.
- Jesse Vincent and contributors, [Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/tree/22f0b463e7a5e579904dd6211a520321035526f7): independent builder comprehension and fresh behavioral evidence.
- GitHub, [Spec Kit](https://github.com/github/spec-kit/tree/ec9a6f3b94c0aa856f77e550785a1ddde1993de1): explicit requirement/scenario IDs, Given/When/Then, and consistency analysis.
