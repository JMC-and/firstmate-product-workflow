# Canonical specification

The executable validator is the schema owner; do not maintain parallel editable specifications.
Generate a versioned structured contract and its readable view from the immutable source chain.
The readable specification includes source inventory, atomic dispositions, exact intent provenance, requirements and significant technical decisions, acceptance plans, journeys, boundaries, external budgets, work assignments and three completion predicates.

Requirements have stable IDs, precise behavior, source-clause references or labelled technical-decision rationale, disposition, acceptance references and lineage.
Source-backed normative requirement text retains the exact source guarantees; put derived implementation detail in build instructions instead of paraphrasing away a restriction.
Acceptance rows describe Given/When/Then, measurable oracle, actual evidence kinds, allowed real/sandbox/synthetic modes, exact environment, planned code/test surfaces and journeys.
Journeys have ordered actions and expected acceptance observations in their environment.
Include a real integrated golden journey; explain every inapplicable alternate, denied, failure or recovery class individually.
A browser-inapplicable boundary needs an explicit reason and an equivalent real public-interface journey.

Do not invent scope to fill a form.
Preserve every original decision, including excluded alternatives.
The owner approves one complete manifest covering exact sources, specification and independent audit.
Changing approved bytes invalidates that manifest.
Adding semantic source material invalidates the current approval and all bindings until a new revision is audited, approved and explicitly rebound.
A pending replacement draft also stops new implementation against the old revision.
