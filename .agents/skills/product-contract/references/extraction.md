# Source extraction

Account for every source event and every non-whitespace character of answer, document, intent and decision text with exact non-overlapping source spans.
Question events retain exact prompts and bindings separately.
Split normative obligations, examples, definitions, constraints, exceptions, anti-goals and unresolved choices into atomic clauses.
Each clause has an explicit disposition and reason.
A normative clause cannot be hidden as contextual.
Required behavior maps bidirectionally to requirements; every requirement cites clauses or a visibly labelled Firstmate technical decision.

Retain accepted, deferred, excluded, contextual and superseded rows rather than deleting inconvenient obligations.
Unresolved rows cannot reach approval.
Exclusions and deferrals remain part of the exact bundle the owner approves, never implementation convenience.
Corrections supersede affected clause and requirement dispositions without rewriting earlier text or recycling IDs.
Preserve unaffected obligations, including when one earlier answer contained several guarantees.
A replacement retains explicit requirement lineage across every original source copy; repeated identical quotes may share one guarantee without losing any provenance link.
Disagreeing answers without an explicit correction or owner resolution stop approval.
Preserve literal source text even when it contains placeholder words; derived required behavior and acceptance instructions must be fully specified.

Character coverage catches missing extraction spans, not semantic weakening.
The independent reverse audit must reread originals rather than trusting this extraction, especially negation, exceptions and multilingual clauses.
It checks for omitted implications, invented requirements, generalized exceptions and contradictory interpretations.
A report may reject a candidate and cause regeneration but cannot approve its own edits.
