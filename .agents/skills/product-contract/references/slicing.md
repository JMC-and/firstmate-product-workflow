# Work slicing

Build independently meaningful vertical work units, not a checklist of layers that silently omits product behavior.
Each unit names its existing task, accepted requirement IDs, all associated journeys, dependencies, instructions and completion level.
Assign every accepted requirement; keep global constraints visible to every worker.
Dependency cycles, unknown references and incomplete journey assignments stop dispatch.

Use a shared approved parent contract across slices.
A slice can be accepted at its promised boundary while other product obligations remain incomplete.
A product-level unit requires aggregate evidence at the integrated candidate, not the conjunction of unrelated slice test results.
Keep actual owner words separate from derived instructions when filling Firstmate's existing scaffold.
The binder compares the exact intent subsection with approved source intent and pins the whole task body against later edits.
[Product Build](../../product-build/SKILL.md) consumes these approved assignments through the existing build checkpoints and lifecycle owners, not a competing scheduler, review pipeline or merger.

A changed product choice needs a source correction and a new approved revision, not a worker-authored scope reduction.
Planned code/test references are implementation guidance; actual proof must name observations, code/test evidence and artifacts at the candidate head.
